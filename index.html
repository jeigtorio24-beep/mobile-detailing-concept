Services

From the seats to the clear coat.

Four services, all mobile. Book the one you need online, or call and talk it through first.

01

Car detailing

A deep clean inside and out: seats, carpets, dash and trims in the cabin, and a careful wash and finish on the paintwork.

02

Ceramic coatings

A hard-wearing protective layer bonded over the paint, so it sheds water and grime more easily and keeps its gloss.

03

Paint correction

Machine polishing that takes out swirl marks and fine scratches, bringing the depth and clarity back to the colour.

04

Window tinting

Tint film fitted to the glass for more privacy, less glare and a cabin that stays cooler on hot Victorian days.
